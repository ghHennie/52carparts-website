How quoting works

  1. You receive a request

    A WhatsApp message with the vehicle, the part and its position, from a client looking for a part you carry.

  2. You quote

    Tap to open the quote form and enter a price for each type you have: OEM, aftermarket, reconditioned or used.

  3. The client chooses

    We show the client the quotes. If yours is chosen, we let you know straight away.

  4. We collect

    Our courier confirms a collection time with you and collects the part from your premises.

Joining

We onboard suppliers by phone: WhatsApp or call us on +27 64 526 0397. We’ll ask which makes and part types you carry, and confirm your business address and contact person.

Your own pace

Quote on the requests that suit you. If you don’t have the part, ignore the request.

Stop any time

Reply STOP to any of our messages and we won’t send you requests again.
