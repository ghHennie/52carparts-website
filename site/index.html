How it works

Everything happens on WhatsApp. No app to install, no account to create.

  1. Send your licence disc

    Send a photo of your licence disc to +27 79 525 5987 on WhatsApp, then complete the short request form.

  2. Get one price

    We ask our suppliers and send you one total, including delivery. Your quote is valid for 48 hours.

  3. Agree delivery

    Accept the quote and agree a delivery date and time with us.

  4. The courier delivers

    A courier collects the part from the supplier and brings it to your door, anywhere in South Africa.

  5. Inspect, then pay

    Accept the part and pay through a secure PayFast link, or reject it and pay nothing.

  • Pay only for what you accept

    You see the part before you pay. Reject it and it costs you nothing.

  • One price, no surprises

    Your quote includes the part and delivery. There are no extra delivery charges.

  • Your details stay private

    Suppliers see your vehicle and the part, never your name, number or address.
