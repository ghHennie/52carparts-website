How it works

  1. Send a photo

    Message us and send a photo of your vehicle licence disc. We read the VIN, engine and registration numbers from it.

  2. Describe the part

    Tell us which part you need, its position, and the types you’d accept: new, aftermarket, reconditioned or used.

  3. Choose a quote

    We ask our suppliers and send you their offers. Every price already includes collection and delivery.

  4. We deliver

    Our courier collects the part from the supplier and brings it to you.

  5. Inspect, then pay

    Check the part. If it’s right, accept it and pay by EFT against your invoice. If it isn’t, reject it and it goes back.

One price, delivery included

The price you accept is the price you pay. Collection and delivery are part of it.

Pay after inspection

You pay by EFT only after you’ve accepted the part. Use the request number on your invoice as the reference.

Everything on WhatsApp

No app to install and no account to create. Every step happens in your WhatsApp chat with us.

Questions

Which areas do you deliver to?
For now, Pretoria only. We’ll add more areas as we grow.
What if I don’t have my licence disc?
You can type your vehicle details instead. The VIN, engine and registration numbers help suppliers find the exact part.
Can I order more than one part?
Each request is for one part. Send a new request for every other part you need.
What if the part is wrong?
Reject it when you inspect it and tell us why. The part goes back to the supplier, and you don’t pay for it.
