About us

52 Car Parts is a brand of Vyf Twee, a South African business. “Vyf twee” is Afrikaans for “five two”: 52.

We started 52 Car Parts because finding a car part still means phoning around, comparing prices that leave out delivery, and taking time off to collect. We bring that onto WhatsApp: you send one request, suppliers quote, and we deliver.

We’re starting small and local, with private clients in Pretoria, a network of trusted suppliers we onboard personally, and our own courier partner. We’ll grow from there.
