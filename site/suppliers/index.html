For parts suppliers in Gauteng

Sell more parts, without the phone calls.

Receive ready-made requests on WhatsApp for the makes and stock you carry. You set your price; we handle the client, the delivery and the payment.

How it works for suppliers

  1. Requests for your stock

    Each request shows the vehicle, VIN, part and part type, only for the makes and stock you carry.

  2. Quote in a short form

    Open the form in WhatsApp and send your price, including VAT if you charge it.

  3. Our courier collects

    If your quote wins, we collect the part. A part the client rejects comes back to you at our cost.

  4. Get paid by EFT

    Within 2 working days after the client’s payment reaches us, with a remittance advice for each request.

  • Your price stays yours

    We add our margin on top of your quote. Nothing is deducted from what you charge.

  • Nothing to install

    Everything runs on WhatsApp, on the phone you already use.

  • Stop at any time

    Reply STOP to any request and you will receive no further messages.
